## Legal notes (NL)
1. **Wft/AFM, mortgages and insurance.** Without an AFM licence the site **may not advise or mediate (bemiddelen)**. Comparing products is often treated as mediation, and a fee per mortgage taken out points to mediation ✅ (afm.nl vergelijkingssites; Daisycon publisher FAQ). Only neutral advertising (banners/links) is allowed. In practice:
   - **Top 5 mortgage rates:** show them as editorial information copied from the lenders' own published rate pages. Include the source link, the date captured, the fixed-rate period and the NHG/LTV class. Sort neutrally (e.g. lowest rate for a stated class) and add the text "geen advies".
   - Avoid words like "beste hypotheek voor jou" and any personal calculator that gives a recommendation. Do **not** put affiliate links inside the rate table.
   - If a rate appears in an *advertisement*, art. 53 BGfo requires the JKP and other cost information ✅ (afm.nl MCD page).
   - Any credit/loan ad must show the official AFM "Geld lenen kost geld" warning ✅ (Ondernemersplein; Daisycon FAQ). The simplest fix is to promote no loans at all.
2. **Affiliate disclosure.** Put a visible note near the links ("Wij ontvangen een vergoeding als je via deze link…") plus a disclosure page (Reclamecode / ACM, the general honesty rule). Label ads "Advertentie".
3. **AVG/GDPR lead forms.** Don't collect leads yourself; send visitors to the advertiser's own form. Any newsletter form needs explicit opt-in (not pre-ticked) and double opt-in, plus a privacy statement naming the controller, purpose, retention and processor. There is no tracking cookie on GitHub Pages itself, but affiliate links set cookies on the advertiser's domain. If you add analytics, use a cookieless tool or a consent banner.
4. **Honesty about the data.** **Do NOT claim "real-time".** CBS prices are monthly and regional, and the CBS itself warns that average prices are not a price indicator ✅ (data.overheid.nl 83625NED). Use wording like "Latest official monthly data (CBS, updated <date>)".
