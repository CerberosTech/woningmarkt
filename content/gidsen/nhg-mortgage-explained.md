# What is NHG?

The Nationale Hypotheek Garantie (NHG, National Mortgage Guarantee) is a guarantee on your mortgage. If circumstances such as job loss, divorce or the death of a partner mean you temporarily cannot pay, NHG can help find solutions. As a last resort, NHG can cancel a remaining debt after the home is sold, subject to conditions.

Because NHG lowers the lender's risk, lenders usually charge a lower rate on NHG loans. According to NHG, more than 40% of Dutch homeowners now have an NHG mortgage.

# The NHG limit in 2026

- The NHG limit in {{year}} is **{{nhgLimit}}**.
- With energy-saving measures it is **{{nhgLimitEBV}}**.
- From 2026 a single limit applies to all home types; the separate limit for mobile homes and pitches has gone.

Since 2023 NHG has set the limit at the average purchase price of the past 27 months, plus 5%, rounded to a multiple of €5,000. The 2027 limit had not been published as of 17 October 2026.

# Cost and benefit

You pay a one-off guarantee fee of **{{nhgFeePct}}** of the amount borrowed, set against the lower rate. NHG itself says the fee is "usually more than recouped" through that lower rate. How much you save depends on the rate gap at your lender, so compare quotes with and without NHG. Current average rates are on [mortgage rates](/en/mortgage-rates/).

NHG does not change how much you may borrow on your income; see [how much can I borrow](how-much-can-i-borrow-netherlands). Without a permanent contract, NHG describes how your income can be established; see [mortgage on a temporary contract](mortgage-temporary-contract).
